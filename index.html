Marin is charting the way to the open frontier of artificial intelligence

To do this, we are discovering and assembling the knowledge required to build artificial intelligence and returning it to the world.

Right now, we're training a 535 billion parameter mixture-of-experts model with 23 billion active parameters, on 18 trillion tokens of data. You can follow along here.

Open means that we share everything:

  • Our discussions (join here)
  • All of our code, including models, infrastructure, etc.
  • Our models' weights
  • The sources of all the data in our training corpus
  • The results of our successful and unsuccessful experiments
  • Tools that make it easy to learn from this effort

Selected writing: 8B dense retro 32B dense retro Delphi scaling suite Cluster scheduling with Iris LLM pretraining efficiency MoE quantile balancing

Marin is primarily developed by Open Athena , with contributors from the community.

This work is generously supported by the Google TPU Research Cloud program for TPUs, the Huang Foundation for GPUs, the Siegel Family Foundation, Fulcrum Science, Schmidt Sciences, and many others. We also benefit immensely from the broader open ecosystem, which has released numerous tools and datasets.

Marin began its voyage in 2024 at Stanford University, created by David Hall and Percy Liang.
